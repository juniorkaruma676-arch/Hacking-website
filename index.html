Important: a lock screen is not a password-recovery screen.

Android does not display a forgotten PIN, pattern or password. Some recovery and reset options erase local data. Do not reset the phone until you know which Google account was previously synced to it and understand the data-loss risk.

Choose the right recovery path

Different locks protect different parts of your device. Start with the problem you actually see.

▦

Forgotten screen lock

Check whether a biometric unlock or an already-authorised trusted unlock method is still available. Otherwise, use the manufacturer's documented recovery process. A reset may erase data.

◎

Google account recovery

Recover the Google account itself using Google's account recovery service. Use a familiar device, browser and location where possible.

🔑

Saved account passwords

View passwords that you previously saved to your own Google Password Manager after signing in and completing Google's identity check. This does not reveal the phone's screen-lock PIN or pattern.

⌑

FRP after flashing

Flashing firmware or resetting through recovery does not remove ownership verification. Complete setup with a Google account previously added and synced to the phone.

FRP: what to do after a flash

Follow these steps when setup asks you to verify a Google account after a reset or firmware flash.

1

Connect to a trusted Wi-Fi network

Keep the phone charged and connected to the internet while completing setup.

2

Use an account previously synced to this phone

Sign in with a Google account that was added to the device before it was reset. A guest account does not satisfy this requirement.

3

If the Google password was forgotten, recover it

Use Google's official recovery page. After a recent password change, Google advises waiting 24 hours before factory resetting; some device support guidance also notes a verification cool-down after a password change.

4

Use proof of ownership for unresolved cases

Contact the phone maker or an authorised service centre with the device and proof of purchase. Ask them to verify ownership and advise on the supported process.

There is no universal FRP code.

FRP is an anti-theft ownership check. A firmware flash, factory reset, or third-party “unlock” utility is not proof of ownership and is not a supported substitute for the previously synced account. Avoid services asking you to send passwords, one-time codes, or remote access to your phone.

Before you factory reset

Take a moment to prevent avoidable data loss and another lockout.

✓

Confirm the Google account

Make sure you know an account that was previously added and synced on the phone. Test signing in on another trusted device first.

✓

Understand what will be erased

A factory reset removes locally stored data, apps and app data. Cloud-synced data may be restored after you sign back in, depending on what was backed up.

✓

Use the manufacturer's instructions

Button combinations and menus vary by exact model. Confirm the model number and use the official support instructions before proceeding.

Frequently asked questions

Can this website show me my forgotten PIN or pattern?

No. The screen-lock PIN, password or pattern is protected by Android and is not readable by a website. For supported options, use the device maker's instructions. A reset may erase local data and can still trigger FRP.

Can I view passwords saved to my Google account?

Yes. Open Google Password Manager while signed in to your own Google Account. Google requires you to sign in again to reveal saved passwords. Only passwords that were previously saved there will appear; this does not reveal a phone's screen-lock PIN, pattern or password.

Will flashing the phone remove FRP?

No. A firmware flash or reset can still leave the device requiring a previously synced Google account during setup. Google's device-protection process requires an eligible account previously added and synced to that device, or its supported screen-lock verification path.

What if I bought the phone second-hand?

Ask the previous owner to complete the account-removal and handover process in person or through the manufacturer's supported process. If that is not possible, contact the manufacturer or an authorised service centre with your proof of purchase.

What if I forgot both the screen lock and Google account?

Start with Google Account Recovery. If you still cannot verify ownership on the device, contact the manufacturer or an authorised service centre with proof of purchase. A reset may erase data and still leave FRP active.

Does DroidCare need my password or unlock code?

No. This page runs locally in your browser and has no login form, password field, analytics tracker or server submission. Use Google's own sign-in screen on passwords.google.com to view saved credentials. Never type your Google password, PIN, pattern or verification code into a recovery guide website.
